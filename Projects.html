NEURO ROADS – Brain Signal Controlled Vehicle (Concept Paper)

Presented as a conceptual research paper in an international conference. The project proposes a system to control vehicles using brain neuro signals transmitted to an Arduino-based interface. The concept is designed to assist physically challenged individuals in operating vehicles independently.

Type: Conceptual Paper Presentation
Domain: Embedded Systems & Assistive Technology
